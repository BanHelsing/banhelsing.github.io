**Period:** 2025-2026

Developed and evaluated a computer-vision regression system in Python using PyTorch, MobileNetV2, transfer learning, and OpenCV to estimate material composition from images.

- Trained the CNN on a 1,275-image dataset.
- Achieved a mean absolute error of 4.92% on the independent test set.
- Substantially outperformed a traditional computer-vision baseline.
